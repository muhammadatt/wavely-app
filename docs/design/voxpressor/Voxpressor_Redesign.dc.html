TURN 2 · ANALOG LAYOUT
Switchable GR meter at the centre, controls arranged around it
Step the METER drum (PEAKS / BODY / PARALLEL) to switch what the needle reads. Knobs drag vertically; the tape-counter drums step with the arrows, a click on a neighbour, or ←/→.
2a Analog faceplate · Modula steel
Peaks and Body flank the needle meter, which switches between Peaks, Body and Parallel via the drum counter below it. The stage the needle is reading lights its title in its colour. De-ess, Parallel, Density and Output (ceiling) sit along the bottom on one baseline. Output pairs compact vertical IN/OUT meters with the ceiling selector — the amber mark on OUT is the ceiling; no trim, the ceiling sets the level.
